## The marks

ZT-DSP, ZT-DSP-Suite and the ZT-DSP logo are marks claimed by
**ZT-DSP Suite maintainers (copyright holder: SCAL-ROAM)**.

Copyright licenses grant no trademark rights:

- AGPL-3.0 section **7(e)** expressly declines to grant them;
- CC BY-SA 4.0 section **2(b)(2)** states that patent and trademark rights are not licensed.

This policy states trademark rights. It is not an extra condition on the copyright
license and does not limit any right that license grants.

## You may

- State factually that your work derives from, implements, or is compatible with ZT-DSP.
- Use the name in a repository title, article, talk, or documentation, provided it
  does not suggest endorsement by the maintainers.
- Distribute unmodified copies and describe them as ZT-DSP.

## You may not, without written permission

- Describe a modified version, fork, or independent implementation as official,
  reference, canonical, or the ZT-DSP implementation.
- Use the ZT-DSP logo as your product icon, or anywhere it implies endorsement.
- Publish a fork to PyPI or crates.io under the names zt-dsp-py, zt_dsp_py, zt-dsp-rs
  or zt_dsp_rs. Those names identify the official distribution.
- Register a domain, package name, organisation name or social account that
  incorporates the marks in a way that implies you are the project.

## Forks: what to do instead

Rename the package, state the derivation in your README, and keep the copyright
notices intact. The license permits that fully; this policy asks for it.

## Reporting misuse

Open an issue in the ZT-DSP-Suite organisation.
